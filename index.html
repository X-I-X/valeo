The Problem

Three Problems That Drain Every Recruiting Team.

Valeo's team is strong. The relationships are real. The problem is what happens around those relationships: the manual work that consumes the hours that should be spent on placements.

The Sourcing Grind
Recruiters spend hours manually searching LinkedIn, job boards, and databases for candidates that match open role profiles. The best candidates are passive; they don't apply. Finding them is manual, slow, and inconsistent.
Follow-Up That Falls Through
A recruiter's pipeline dies when follow-up is inconsistent. Candidates go cold. Clients stop hearing from you. Placements slip to competitors who called back faster. Every missed follow-up is a missed fee.
Knowledge That Walks Out the Door
Twenty years of placement patterns, candidate relationships, and client intelligence lives in people's heads. Recruiter turnover in staffing runs 35–40% annually. Every departure takes the playbook with it.

Solution Architecture

Four Layers That Connect Execution to Intelligence.

Already using a CRM? Tribune doesn't replace it. Tribune turns it into an agentic operating system , layering intelligence and automation on top of the workflows you already have.

L1
Make.com / Workflow Automations
Pipeline Execution Layer
Deterministic workflows that run 24/7: candidate outreach sequences (Day 1→3→7→14), interview reminders, weekly client updates, recertification reactivation, new role intake triggers. Every follow-up happens on schedule. Nothing falls through the cracks.
L2
OpenClaw / Agentic AI: Mission Control Terminal
Recruiter Intelligence Layer
Morning briefings with daily priority list. At-risk placement alerts. Candidate scoring against open roles. Call prep before every client touch. Recruiters stop managing from memory and start managing from a live command center.
L3
Knowledge Intelligence Engine
Talent Intelligence Layer
Valeo's 20 years of placement history becomes a living competitive advantage. Which role types fill fastest? Which candidate sources have the highest 6-month retention? Which BH clinic segments have the highest repeat business? The institutional knowledge compounds, and stays when people move on.

C-Suite Agent Layer
BD + Strategy Layer
Rob's personal intelligence engine. Monitors when BH clinics in Valeo's markets post new jobs (signals they're growing or struggling to hire). Tracks relationship cadence for every key contact. Weekly BD briefing every Monday. Auto-drafts outreach for Rob to review.
